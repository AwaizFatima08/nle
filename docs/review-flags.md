## Sources for the exam pattern
- PM&DC, National Registration Examination (Medical) Standard, Structure and Syllabus 2024 (pmdc.pk/Documents/Syllabus/, read 4 Nov 2026): 200 MCQs, 70 % clinical / 30 % basic, subject counts, 60 % pass, no negative marking, Angoff, 5-year validity, CSE 20 stations, 8 + 2 minutes, 3 mandatory stations.
- The 2024 basic-science table sums to 56, not the stated 60 (Pathology 11, Anatomy 11, Physiology 10, Pharmacology 8, Biochemistry 6, Community Medicine 6, Forensic 4). The 2023 standard had 16/12/10/8/6/6/2 = 60. Our mocks use 13/13/10/8/6/6/4. Change in `tool/make_blueprint.py` if PM&DC publishes a corrected table.
- Theory duration: not in the PM&DC standard. 3 hours 30 minutes is reported by prep providers (bilalmd.com); mocks use 210 minutes.
- Dates: a third-party site (highyield.pk) reports Step 1 on 8 November 2026 (moved from 11 October by a PM&DC notice of 5 October). Not used in the app.
