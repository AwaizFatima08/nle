## Sources for the exam pattern
- PM&DC, National Registration Examination (Medical) Standard, Structure and Syllabus 2024 (pmdc.pk/Documents/Syllabus/, read 4 Nov 2026): 200 MCQs, 70 % clinical / 30 % basic, subject counts, 60 % pass, no negative marking, Angoff, 5-year validity, CSE 20 stations, 8 + 2 minutes, 3 mandatory stations.
- The 2024 basic-science table sums to 56, not the stated 60 (Pathology 11, Anatomy 11, Physiology 10, Pharmacology 8, Biochemistry 6, Community Medicine 6, Forensic 4).
