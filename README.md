# Prep Simulator for NLE

A free Android app by [HomiLabs](https://homilabs.org) for foreign medical graduates preparing for the PM&DC National Licensing Examination (NLE, now the National Registration Examination, NRE) Step 1 theory paper: 10 full-length 200-question mock papers with scoring and explanations, subject practice, and exam rules, guidelines and tips. Independent practice material, not affiliated with PM&DC.

- App: Flutter, in `lib/`. Project notes: `CLAUDE.md`.
- Question bank: `content/bank/`, built into `assets/data/` by `tool/build_content.py`.
- Website: `website/`, live at https://nle.homilabs.org.
- Release and hosting: `docs/release-and-hosting.md`.
